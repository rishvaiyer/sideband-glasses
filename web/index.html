  1. Your iPhone listens. The native app observes Bluetooth advertisements while open.

Know the edges.

Quiet devices may not be visible. Bluetooth addresses can change. Sideband does not locate people, detect all cameras, determine who is recording, or declare a place safe. The browser experience is a simulation; real scanning requires the native iPhone app.
